# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to help.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Taking a disagreement to the evidence. This project scores websites, and
  people will disagree with a score. "That rule fires on hand-built pages too,
  here are three" is worth more than any amount of certainty.
- Accepting that a measured result beats a strongly held opinion, including
  a maintainer's.
- Being specific about what broke, and on what.

Behaviour that does not:

- Personal attacks, insults, or comments about someone's identity
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption, or deliberately wasting other people's time

## Scope

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages and code review, and when representing the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report anything that troubles you to **hello@lumioguard.dev**. Reports are
read by the maintainers and kept confidential. You will get a reply within three
working days.

If your report concerns a maintainer, say so; it will be handled by someone
else.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and will
explain why when they do. Repeated or serious breaches lead to a temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions are made by people, so they will sometimes be wrong. If
you think one was, say so at the address above.
